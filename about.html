Built on good work and straight answers.

JI Roofing & Exteriors Corp is a local, owner-operated roofing company protecting homes across Long Island, Queens and Brooklyn.

Our story

Two decades of Long Island roofs

JI Roofing & Exteriors Corp started the way most good trades companies do — one truck, one crew, and a refusal to cut corners where nobody would see.

Twenty years later we're still owner-operated. We've re-roofed capes in Levittown, slate estates on the North Shore, flat-roofed row houses in Brooklyn and salt-battered shingle homes in the Rockaways. Every one of them taught us something about how water actually moves on a roof.

What hasn't changed: the owner still walks every estimate, we still install systems rather than just shingles, and we still leave your property cleaner than we found it.

What we stand for

The standards we refuse to bend

These aren't marketing lines — they're the rules our crews work by every day.

Diagnose before you sell

If a $400 flashing repair solves your problem, we'll tell you — even when a $20,000 roof is on the table.

Install the full system

Starter strip, ice & water shield, synthetic underlayment, new flashing, ridge vent and cap — not just the shingle you can see.

Put it in writing

Material brands, product lines, colors, scope and price — all itemized before anyone climbs a ladder.

Show up when we say

Scheduled start dates we actually keep, and a phone that gets answered by a human being.

Protect the property

Tarps on landscaping, plywood on siding, magnetic sweeps daily. Your yard should look untouched.

Stand behind it

Workmanship warranty from us, system warranty from the manufacturer, and a callback policy that means something.
