About JI Roofing

A local roofing company that actually picks up the phone

JI Roofing & Exteriors Corp was built on a simple idea: treat every house like it's your own, explain the work in plain English, and never leave a homeowner guessing.

We've spent two decades on Long Island roofs — the salt air in Long Beach, the wind off the Sound in Northport, the nor'easters that find every weak flashing in Queens and Brooklyn. That experience shows up in the details: proper ice & water shield coverage, balanced ridge-and-soffit ventilation, and flashing that's replaced rather than reused.
