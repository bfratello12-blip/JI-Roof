Roof Installation

New roof systems built for the Northeast

Whether it's a new build, an addition, a dormer or a garage, we install complete roof systems engineered for Long Island's wind loads, snow loads and coastal salt exposure — not just a layer of shingles over whatever is there.

  • Deck inspection, re-nailing and plywood replacement as needed
  • Ice & water shield at eaves, valleys and penetrations
  • Synthetic underlayment, new drip edge and step flashing
  • Balanced ridge-and-soffit ventilation sized to your attic
  • GAF
  • CertainTeed
  • Owens Corning
  • New construction
  • Additions
Roof Replacement

Full tear-offs done right in one to two days

We don't shingle over failing roofs. A proper replacement means stripping to the deck so we can see — and fix — what's actually underneath. Most homes are finished in a single day, start to broom-clean.

  • Complete tear-off to bare decking with on-site dumpster
  • Rotted sheathing identified and replaced before install
  • All flashing replaced — never reused or caulked over
  • Daily magnetic nail sweeps and full property protection
  • Tear-off
  • Storm damage
  • Insurance claims
  • Financing available
Roof Repair

Find the leak. Fix the leak. That's it.

Most leaks aren't a dead roof — they're a failed flashing, a cracked boot, a lifted ridge cap or an ice-dam scar. We trace water to its real source and repair what's broken instead of selling you a roof you don't need yet.

  • Leak detection with attic and surface inspection
  • Chimney, skylight, valley and pipe-boot flashing repair
  • Wind and storm damage shingle replacement
  • Emergency tarping to stop water today
  • Emergency service
  • Leak tracing
  • Flashing
  • Ice dams
Asphalt Roofing

The best value on Long Island, installed to spec

Architectural asphalt shingles give you 30–50 year rated performance, dozens of colors and the strongest warranties in the business. We install GAF Timberline®, CertainTeed Landmark® and Owens Corning Duration® systems.

  • Architectural, designer and impact-rated shingle options
  • Full manufacturer system installs for extended warranty eligibility
  • Algae-resistant shingles for shaded and coastal properties
  • Color consultation with samples at your home
  • GAF Timberline
  • CertainTeed Landmark
  • Owens Corning Duration
Flat Roofing

Low-slope systems that actually drain

Rear extensions, porch roofs, dormers, garages and Brooklyn row houses all need low-slope solutions. We install fully adhered and mechanically fastened membrane systems with proper tapered insulation and drainage.

  • TPO, EPDM rubber and modified bitumen systems
  • Tapered insulation to eliminate ponding water
  • Parapet, scupper and drain detailing
  • Commercial and multi-family capability
  • TPO
  • EPDM
  • Modified bitumen
  • Commercial
Cedar Wood Roofing

Real cedar, installed the traditional way

Cedar is unforgiving of shortcuts. It needs the right exposure, the right fasteners and the right ventilation underneath. We hand-select grade-A red cedar shakes and shingles and install them so they weather beautifully instead of failing early.

  • Grade-A red cedar shakes and shingles
  • Breathable underlayment for proper back-ventilation
  • Stainless fasteners for coastal properties
  • Fire-treated and preservative-treated options
  • Hand-split shakes
  • Perfection shingles
  • Coastal rated
  • Historic homes
Metal Roofing

A roof you install once

Standing seam and metal shingle roofs routinely last 50+ years, shed snow, resist wind uplift and reflect summer heat. We fabricate panels to your roof's exact dimensions for clean, hidden-fastener lines.

  • Standing seam, metal shingle and corrugated profiles
  • Hidden fastener systems — no exposed screws to fail
  • Kynar® finish colors with long-term fade warranties
  • Snow guards and ice-retention systems where needed
  • Standing seam
  • 50+ year life
  • Energy efficient
  • Wind rated
Copper & Custom Work

Hand-formed copper that becomes the detail people notice

Copper is where craftsmanship shows. We fabricate and install bay window roofs, dormer caps, finials, standing seam copper panels, half-round gutters and custom flashing that will outlive the rest of the house.

  • Copper bay windows, porticos, dormers and turrets
  • Standing seam and flat-lock copper panels
  • Half-round copper gutters and round leaders
  • Custom chimney caps, crickets and valley pans
  • Custom fabrication
  • Historic restoration
  • Half-round gutters
  • Architectural detail
Slate Roofing

Century roofs, installed and restored

Slate is the longest-lived roof there is — when it's handled by someone who understands it. We install new natural and synthetic slate and carefully restore existing slate roofs with color- and thickness-matched replacements.

  • Natural Vermont, Spanish and synthetic slate
  • Matched individual slate replacement and re-hooking
  • Copper flashing, valleys and snow guards
  • Structural assessment for slate-ready framing
  • Natural slate
  • Synthetic slate
  • Restoration
  • Estate homes
Composite Roofing

The look of cedar or slate, engineered to last

Composite shake and slate give you the architectural character of natural materials with Class 4 impact resistance, Class A fire ratings and no splitting, curling or moss. Ideal when an HOA or historic district wants the look without the maintenance.

  • Synthetic shake and synthetic slate profiles
  • Class 4 impact and Class A fire rated options
  • 50-year limited manufacturer warranties
  • Lightweight — no structural reinforcement required
  • Class 4 impact
  • Low maintenance
  • HOA friendly
  • 50-year warranty
Gutters, Leaders & Downspouts

Move water away from your foundation

Undersized or clogged gutters cause more damage than most leaks — rotted fascia, ice dams, wet basements and cracked walkways. We run seamless gutter on site, size it to your roof's actual water load, and can add Leaf Guard protection so you stop climbing ladders.

  • Seamless 5" and oversized 6" aluminum, formed on site
  • Copper and half-round gutter for premium homes
  • Leaf Guard and gutter-cover systems
  • Leaders, downspout extensions and underground tie-ins
  • Leaf Guard
  • Seamless aluminum
  • Copper gutters
  • Fascia & soffit
